## Type
- One font, Baloo 2, from `src/app/fonts/Baloo2-latin.woff2`, declared at 400, 700 and 800 (`src/app/layout.tsx:19-26`) as `--font`, used by `body` (`globals.css:185`).
- Digits are tabular everywhere (`globals.css:187`). `h1`, `h2` and `h3` balance their lines, `p` avoids a lone last word (`globals.css:321-333`). `h1` and `h2` are 800 (`globals.css:316-319`).
- Every font size is in rem, 16px = 1rem, so text follows the player's browser text size; the px sizes in this file are at the browser's default (docs/milestones/M14-14b-17-lines-and-text-size.md). Paddings, gaps, borders, the 44px controls and the breakpoints stay px; the badge and the avatar slots are in rem too, so they grow with their letters (`kit.module.css:175-189`, `src/app/avatar.module.css`).
- A paragraph stops at 55ch, about 70 letters in Baloo 2 (`globals.css:328-333`). On the reading pages (Privacy, About and signed-out Today, marked `.reading`) a list item stops there too and an `h2` has 40px above it and 10px below (`globals.css:335-345`). A word too long for its box breaks (`globals.css:188-190`).
- Inputs and buttons are 16px (`globals.css:388-392`). A card's heading is 24px (`src/app/kit.module.css:30-33`); a chip and a badge are 12px (`kit.module.css:134`, `:187`).
- The numbers in stats are 40px where they fit, else a share of their cell's width (the cell is a size container, `src/app/kit.module.css:170`): Me's totals 24% (`src/app/me.module.css:68`), the game screen's stats 35% (`src/app/offbeat.module.css:95`); so is the number in `/streak`'s heading (`src/app/streak.module.css:18-20`).

## Corners, borders, shadows
- The ink of borders and shadows is the theme's text colour: `--ink: var(--text)` (`globals.css:366`).
- Corners: `--r-card` 5px, `--r-button` 5px, `--r-small` 4px, `--r-cell` 2px (`globals.css:367-370`).
- Only what can be pressed has a shadow: `--shadow-button` `0 4px 0` and `--shadow-button-small` `0 3px 0`, in the ink (`globals.css:371-372`). Cards, rows, chips, stats, the current tab and the streak pill are flat.
- One control height, 44px: fields and selects have a 2px ink border and the small corners (`globals.css:395-402`), at least 44px tall with 0 12px inside (`globals.css:406-411`); a text area keeps 10px 12px (`globals.css:413-415`). Their text is 16px, a text area's and a select's too (`globals.css:379-385`).
- Buttons are at least 44px tall, with a 2px ink border, the button corners and shadow (`globals.css:426-440`), the kit's kinds included (no vertical padding: `kit.module.css:206-247`); pressed, they drop 4px onto the shadow, which goes, in 80 ms (`globals.css:452-456`), and don't move under reduced motion (`globals.css:459-469`). A disabled button, of any kind, is flat: a `--edge` border on `--surface`, `--muted` text (`globals.css:474-480`). A link drawn as a button is 44px too (`src/app/button-link.module.css:10`). The reaction chips are the one exception until they move behind a menu (14b-10, answer 18).
- The focus ring is 3px in the accent, 3px out (`globals.css:352-355`).

## Spacing and layout
- A page's content is one column at most 960px wide with 16px padding (`globals.css:309-314`).
- Nothing pushes the page sideways when text is bigger: a card's head lets its chip drop under the heading (`kit.module.css:35-45`), a chip's text wraps (`:123-136`), a week's head on the leaderboard graph wraps (`src/app/race.module.css:194-201`), a board's row drops its points under the name (`src/app/leaderboard.module.css:77-90`), though at the default text size a long name is cut with an ellipsis and the points stay on its row, from a viewport 20em wide (`leaderboard.module.css:97-114`), and Today's column is as wide as the screen (`src/app/today.module.css:7`).
- **Below 768px:** a 56px top bar (`src/app/shell.module.css:25-32`) and a 64px tab bar at the foot with a 2px ink top rule (`shell.module.css:3-13`); the page leaves that room (`globals.css:210-213`), and what it scrolls to, by focus or a script, stops above the tab bar (and the clock bar), not under it: `--room-bottom` is the root's scroll padding (`globals.css:201-208`).
- **From 768px:** a 280px sidebar with a 2px ink right rule and no top bar (`shell.module.css:180-197`, `globals.css:254-265`).
- **From 1200px:** a 360px right column (`src/app/side-column.tsx:21`, `src/app/side-column.module.css:10`, `globals.css:278-281`).
- The streak pill in the top bar and the sidebar is fully round, in the soft tint, with a 1.5px ink border (`shell.module.css:58-72`); it looks 34px tall and is a 44px-tall target, through a `::after` centred on it (`:74-80`).
- During a run, the clock bar is 48px with a 2px ink rule (`shell.module.css:327-343`), above the tab bar on phones and at the top from 768px (`globals.css:249-251`, `:284-287`).
- Detail pages slide 60px over 200 ms, forward to the left and back to the right, and don't move under reduced motion (`globals.css:486-515`, `:577-584`).

- The shell (the `main`, its "Plor" `h1`, the frame with the top bar, tab bar or sidebar, and the footer) is one component, `Shell` in `src/app/shell-page.tsx` (`:14-29`; not the navigation's `Shell` in `src/app/shell.tsx`), drawn by the player pages' layout (`src/app/(shell)/layout.tsx`) and the 404 page.

## Motion
- One easing and time: `--ease` `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)` and `--quick` 200ms (`globals.css:373-376`). Under reduced motion nothing moves; a state still changes, at once.
- **Buttons**, on a device that hovers, lift 2px and their shadow grows to 6px, through `--shadow-button` (`globals.css:442-450`); a small one's to 5px (`kit.module.css:224-228`); a link drawn as a button the same (`button-link.module.css:25-30`). The reactions' ⋯ and its popover's items don't lift (`src/app/reactions.module.css:36-51`, `:82-91`).
- **Rows** take the soft tint under the pointer (`kit.module.css:90-105`).
- **The reactions' popover** pops in from 90%, from its top right corner (`reactions.tsx:107`, `reactions.module.css:66-75`); its emoji and actions take the soft tint under the pointer (`reactions.module.css:120-130`).
- **Tab icons** have two versions, an outline and a solid one that does something (Today's calendar peels a corner, Friends goes ^ ^ with a grin, Groups turns point-down, Me gains a sparkle), in the tab's own colour, muted at rest and the text colour hovered or current, as its label is (`src/app/shell.tsx:39-164`). The current tab shows the solid one; from 768px a hovered tab takes the soft fill and the solid one, which Groups turns 60° into (`shell.module.css:128-163`, `:251-279`).
- **Count-ups:** points and streaks (Me's totals, the game screen's streak and best score, the streak pill) count to their value over 600ms when it differs from what this browser last showed (`src/app/shown.ts:9`, `:53-79`, `src/app/count-up.tsx`).
- **Pops:** a reaction added, a comment that arrives and a new streak rank come in from 90% of their size (`kit.module.css:268-284`).
