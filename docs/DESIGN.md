# Design, as built

What Plor's interface is made of today, read from the code. Each value names the file and line that holds it. It records no reasons and no history; a PR that changes what it describes updates it (CLAUDE.md, Docs).

## Rules
Every screen builds from these; a piece not named here is added here and to the kit first (docs/milestones/M20-design-rules-and-clean-up.md, answer 2). Each piece is drawn at `/admin/kit` (`src/app/admin/kit/kit.tsx`), under the name it has here.

**A calm frame, loud moments** (M20, answer 21). The **frame** is what a player reads and moves through: the sidebar and tab bar, page headings, comments, member and friend lists, settings, the gaps between cards. It stays plain. The **moments** are what comes from playing: the game cards, results, scores, board rows, friends' results in the feed, rank changes, reactions. Only a moment may use the levers (answer 18): its game's colour, a bigger size, a motion. No art and no second font. Which look the moments take is chosen on `/admin/kit` (20k).

- **The hover rule** (`kit.module.css:97-129`, `globals.css:466-487`): anything pressable that isn't the main button takes the `--soft` fill and the `--text` colour under the pointer, over `--quick` with `--ease`; under reduced motion the state still changes, at once. Every button has it from `globals.css`, at no specificity so a button's own class wins (a module can't name an element alone); rows, links drawn as buttons and anything else take it from the kit (`.row`, `.hover`), as the sidebar and tab-bar tabs, the sidebar's Admin and every `ButtonLink` do (M20, answer 37). An icon with an outline and a solid version swaps them too: the tabs and the sidebar's Settings, whose solid gear is the outline filled with its middle cut out (`src/app/shell.tsx`, `GEAR_ART`; `shell.module.css`; M20 answer 48).
- **The focus ring**, everywhere: 3px in the accent, 3px out (`globals.css:352-355`).
- **Gaps:** 16px between cards on every page (M20, answer 11).
- **Each screen's main action** (the raised `.primary`, at most one in view on a player screen; M20, answers 45 to 47 and 50): Today, the Play or Continue of the card in front, the game left to play (`today-games.ts`), a finished card's Result flat; signed out, the front card's "Play without an account", or the form's button once the sign-in form is open (M20 20n-7); Groups, Create group; a group's page, Share; joining a group, Join group; a profile, Add friend; sign-in, "Email me a sign-in code", then "Sign in", then the username's Save; Settings, Save while a name is being edited; How to play, Next; the game screens, Start, Answer or Try, Next and See results; a result, Share; the 404, Go to Today; the admin's Puzzles, each game's Approve, both in view (answer 50), and Base, Look up. Friends, Me, Streak and the feed have none: Post and Find are flat.
- **A game's card** (M20 20k-3, answers 54, 55 and 57; the Balanced look): the kit's white card, centred, with a band in its game's colour across its top holding its emblem, its name (the card's `h2`) and the day; its chips are the kit's own and its buttons the ordinary ones. Once played, the score is its big number, 1.875rem 800 with its unit at 1rem, popping in (`src/app/game.tsx`, `Banner`, `Band`, `BigScore`; `game.module.css`, `.banner`, `.band`, `.score`). It is Today's two cards and the top of each game's own screen once its run is over: Offbeat's `Banner`, Fragments' card's band and big score (`src/app/substrings.tsx`).

## Type
- One font, Baloo 2, from `src/app/fonts/Baloo2-latin.woff2`, declared at 400, 700 and 800 (`src/app/layout.tsx:19-26`) as `--font`, used by `body` (`globals.css:185`).
- Digits are tabular everywhere (`globals.css:187`). `h1`, `h2` and `h3` balance their lines, `p` avoids a lone last word (`globals.css:321-333`). `h1` and `h2` are 800 (`globals.css:316-319`).
- Every font size is in rem, 16px = 1rem, so text follows the player's browser text size; the px sizes in this file are at the browser's default (docs/milestones/M14-14b-17-lines-and-text-size.md). Paddings, gaps, borders, the 44px controls and the breakpoints stay px; the badge and the avatar slots are in rem too, so they grow with their letters (`kit.module.css:194-208`, `src/app/avatar.module.css`).
- A paragraph stops at 55ch, about 70 letters in Baloo 2 (`globals.css:328-333`). On the reading pages (Privacy, About and signed-out Today, marked `.reading`) a list item stops there too and an `h2` has 40px above it and 10px below (`globals.css:335-345`). A word too long for its box breaks (`globals.css:188-190`).
- Inputs are 16px (`globals.css:414-418`); a button is 14px, the main one 17px (`globals.css:453-464`, `kit.module.css:235-254`). A card's heading is 24px (`src/app/kit.module.css:30-33`); a chip and a badge are 12px (`kit.module.css:153`, `:206`).
- The numbers in stats are 40px where they fit, else a share of their cell's width (the cell is a size container, `src/app/kit.module.css:189`): Me's totals 24% (`src/app/me.module.css:68`), the game screen's stats 35% (`src/app/offbeat.module.css:95`); so is the number in `/streak`'s heading (`src/app/streak.module.css:18-20`).

## Corners, borders, shadows
- The ink of borders and shadows is the theme's text colour: `--ink: var(--text)` (`globals.css:366`).
- Corners: `--r-card` 5px, `--r-button` 5px, `--r-small` 4px, `--r-cell` 2px (`globals.css:367-370`).
- Only the main action has a shadow, its 4px edge: `--shadow-button` `0 4px 0`, in the ink (`globals.css:398`). Every other button, cards, rows, chips, stats, the current tab and the streak pill are flat (M20, answers 6 and 45). An inset shadow draws a flat stripe, not a shadow: a feed result's game-colour edge (M20 20h).
- One control height, 44px: fields and selects have a 2px ink border and the small corners (`globals.css:395-402`), at least 44px tall with 0 12px inside (`globals.css:406-411`); a text area keeps 10px 12px (`globals.css:413-415`). Their text is 16px, a text area's and a select's too (`globals.css:379-385`).
- Buttons are 44px tall: a flat one is 44px (`globals.css:453-464`); the main one is a 40px face on its 4px edge (`kit.module.css:235-254`; M20 answer 11), drops 4px onto its edge in 80 ms when pressed (`kit.module.css:263-269`), and doesn't move under reduced motion. A disabled button, of any kind, is flat: a `--edge` border on `--surface`, `--muted` text (`globals.css:491-497`). A link drawn as a button is 44px too (`src/app/button-link.module.css:4-18`). The reaction chips are the one exception until they move behind a menu (14b-10, answer 18).
- The focus ring is 3px in the accent, 3px out (`globals.css:352-355`).

## Spacing and layout
- A page's content is one column at most 960px wide with 16px padding (`globals.css:309-314`).
- Nothing pushes the page sideways when text is bigger: a card's head lets its chip drop under the heading (`kit.module.css:35-45`), a chip's text wraps (`:123-136`), a week's head on the leaderboard graph wraps (`src/app/race.module.css:194-201`), a board's row drops its points under the name (`src/app/leaderboard.module.css:77-90`), though at the default text size a long name is cut with an ellipsis and the points stay on its row, from a viewport 20em wide (`leaderboard.module.css:97-114`), and Today's column is as wide as the screen (`src/app/today.module.css:7`).
- **Below 768px:** a 56px top bar (`src/app/shell.module.css:25-32`) and a 64px tab bar at the foot with a 2px ink top rule (`shell.module.css:3-13`); the page leaves that room (`globals.css:210-213`), and what it scrolls to, by focus or a script, stops above the tab bar (and the clock bar), not under it: `--room-bottom` is the root's scroll padding (`globals.css:201-208`).
- **From 768px:** a 280px sidebar with a 2px ink right rule and no top bar (`shell.module.css:180-197`, `globals.css:254-265`). The wordmark's letters, the avatar, the tab icons and the Settings gear share one left edge, 28px in: the tabs' padding is the wordmark's 12px less their 2px border, the icons sit at their own width, and Settings takes the 12px (`shell.module.css:239-250`, `:294`; M20 answer 42).
- **From 1200px:** a 360px right column (`src/app/side-column.tsx:21`, `src/app/side-column.module.css:10`, `globals.css:278-281`).
- The streak pill in the top bar and the sidebar is fully round, in the soft tint, with a 1.5px ink border (`shell.module.css:58-72`); it is the avatar's height, 1.875rem, so the two line up beside each other (`shell.module.css:71-72`; M20 answer 40), and is a 44px-tall target, through a `::after` centred on it.
- During a run, the clock bar is 48px with a 2px ink rule (`shell.module.css:327-343`), above the tab bar on phones and at the top from 768px (`globals.css:249-251`, `:284-287`).
- Detail pages slide 60px over 200 ms, forward to the left and back to the right, and don't move under reduced motion (`globals.css:486-515`, `:577-584`).
