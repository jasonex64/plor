Plor logo: the peeking o, with a point inside

The outside is a plain circle. The white inside rises into one small soft point that leans the way he looks. Three heights of point, A to C; nothing else changes. Each is shown on Paper and on Night: alone at 16, 32, 180 and 512 px, as an app icon with the maskable safe circle (radius 40% of the icon's width) dotted over it, and as the o in the word, at sidebar size and large.

At 16 and 32 px and in the sidebar word he is redrawn so the point holds up: a thinner ring, a bigger white, bigger eyes and a taller point. The app icon is the same drawing as the mark, a blue ring on a white square, not white on blue: on a blue square his face has to turn blue and stops looking like cream. That blue version is last in each icon row, for comparison.

My pick is B: its point is tall enough to still show at 16 px, and low enough that he stays a round o with a soft peak.
