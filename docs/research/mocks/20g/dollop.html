Plor logo: the peeking o with a dollop on top

Four versions of the same character, A to D, from least to most dollop. Each is shown on Paper and on Night: alone at 16, 32, 180 and 512 px, as an app icon with the maskable safe circle (radius 40% of the icon's width) dotted over it, and twice with the word as it would sit at the top of the 280 px sidebar, once standing in for the o and once beside the word. Rough on purpose: react to the shapes, not the finish.
