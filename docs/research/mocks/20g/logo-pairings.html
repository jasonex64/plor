Plor logo: six first sketches

Each sketch is a mark and the word redrawn to go with it, on Paper and on Night. Marks are shown at 16, 32, 180 and 512 px, then as an app icon with the maskable safe circle (radius 40% of the icon's width, from web.dev) dotted over it, then with the word as it would sit at the top of the 280 px sidebar. Rough on purpose: react to the ideas, not the finish.
