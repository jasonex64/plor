## Goal
The Me tab shows the player's own stats: totals across games, this month's calendar of days played, a card for Offbeat and their rarest answers (L45). Avatar slots sit by every name (L37), and the Friends tab gets L31's friendlier empty state.

## Who uses it / what breaks without it
- **Used by:** every player who opens Me, and everyone who sees a name on a board, in a comment or in a group's member list.
- **Breaks without it:**
  - Me is still the account page, while L45 made it "mainly show your individual stats".
  - Nothing shows the totals, the history or the rarest answers.
  - The avatars ticket (L37) has no slots to fill.
  - The Friends tab's empty text is the old one L31 asked to replace.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Totals across games.** Me shows:
   - points all time (the ledger, settled first, as `allTimePoints`);
   - days played (days with a finished run);
   - the day streak now (`getStreak`);
   - the longest streak (the longest run of puzzle days played, counted as the streak is).

   Checked by a db test of `GET /api/me` with numbers worked by hand.
2. **This month's calendar.**
   - The current month by `private.today()`, Sunday first.
   - Each day with a finished run is shaded by questions survived, in four steps (0–1, 2–3, 4–5, 6–7).
   - A day before today without a finished run is outlined; today is outlined thick; later days are dashed.
   - Above it, "N of M days", where M is the days so far this month, today included.
   - A text alternative says the same.

   Checked by the db test (each day's value) and `e2e/me.e2e.ts` (the shades and the label).
3. **Offbeat's card:** the best score and the average survived, as on the game screen; full climbs (finished runs that survived all 7); and a link to the game screen. Checked by the db test and `e2e/me.e2e.ts`.
4. **Your rarest answers.**
   - The player's 3 answers with the lowest share among the question's players, before that day's midnight.
   - Only from closed days, and only where at least `ANSWER_SHARE_MIN_PLAYERS` answered.
   - Each is shown with its question, its share ("under 1% gave this" / "N% gave this", as on the result) and its date.
   - No answers: none shown, with a line saying so.

   Checked by the db test (with 5 players on a closed day, and a day under the minimum that's left out) and in `e2e/me.e2e.ts`.
5. **Me's header:** the avatar slot (large), the username and "Playing since" with the profile's date. Checked in `e2e/me.e2e.ts`.
6. **Avatar slots:** a round slot holding the initial, hidden from screen readers since the name is beside it, by every name in:
   - Me's header;
   - the live board;
   - the leaderboards;
   - the comments;
   - a group's member list.

   Checked in `e2e/me.e2e.ts`.
7. **Friends' empty state.** With no friends, the Friends tab's board says round 3's "Plor is better with friends" and its line, with the invite link as now. Checked in `e2e/me.e2e.ts`.
8. `npm run lint`, `npx tsc --noEmit -p .`, `npm test`, `npm run test:db`, `npm run test:e2e` and CI pass.
